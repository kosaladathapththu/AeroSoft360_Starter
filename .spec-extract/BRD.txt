1. Purpose and Background
Supun Aerosoft currently carries out key operational and financial activities through a mixture of existing software, Excel workbooks and manual formats. The company requires one connected system that gives management a reliable view of production, stock, sales, collections, suppliers, payroll, commissions, banking and accounts without repeatedly preparing separate reports.
Business requirement in one sentence
Create a centralized, multi-user business system that records each transaction once and automatically carries the same information through stock, production, sales, finance, payroll and management reporting.
2. Business Objectives
Reduce repeated manual data entry and Excel-based calculations.
Give management one-shot dashboards for production, stock, purchase orders, sales, collections, payables, cash flow and payroll.
Maintain accurate raw material, work-in-progress and finished-goods balances.
Trace customer orders from PO to sales order, invoice, receipt and return.
Calculate sales-representative commission from actual customer collections within the company's 100-day rule.
Import attendance data from the existing attendance system and automate the company's current salary calculations.
Provide audit trails, role-based access and reliable financial records for bank, petty cash, taxes, expenses and journals.
Allow multiple authorized users to work on the same centralized online database.

6.6 Sales Commission
ID
Requirement
BR-COM-01
Commission eligibility shall be based on customer collections received within 100 days from the invoice date.
BR-COM-02
For an eligible collection, the system shall calculate Payment Percentage = Received/Allocated Amount ÷ Invoice Amount.
BR-COM-03
Base Commission shall be calculated from the applicable product/category quantity and approved commission rate: Base Commission = Quantity × Rate.
BR-COM-04
Final Commission shall be calculated as: (Quantity × Rate) × (Received/Allocated Amount ÷ Invoice Amount). The calculation shall be performed using the applicable invoice line/category values and collection allocation.
BR-COM-05
If the applicable collection is received after 100 days, commission for that late collection shall be zero. Returns/credit notes affecting the invoice shall be considered before final commission approval.
BR-COM-06
Commission rate shall be configurable by the approved business rule (for example product/category/rep/channel) and the report shall show invoice amount, received amount, payment percentage, quantity, rate, days taken and final commission.

7. Confirmed Business Rules
7.1 Commission Rule
Invoice issued and assigned to sales representative
↓
Customer receipt recorded and allocated to the invoice
↓
System calculates days from invoice date to receipt date and Payment % = Received/Allocated Amount ÷ Invoice Amount
↓
If collection is within 100 days: Base Commission = Quantity × Approved Rate
↓
Final Commission = (Quantity × Rate) × Payment Percentage
↓
If collection is after 100 days: Commission for that late collection = Rs. 0
Calculation Example
Invoice Amount = Rs. 10,000 | Received Amount = Rs. 5,000 | Quantity = 10 | Rate = Rs. 50 | Days = 90
Payment Percentage = 5,000 ÷ 10,000 = 50%
Base Commission = 10 × 50 = Rs. 500
Final Commission = 500 × 50% = Rs. 250

If the same collection is received after 100 days, the commission for that late collection is Rs. 0. For partial/multiple receipts, the calculation is applied to the amount allocated to the invoice, so commission is earned only in proportion to the amount collected within the eligible period.

7.3 Attendance and Payroll Rules Identified from the July 2026 Workbook
Rule
Current workbook logic / observed value
System treatment
Normal start / late start
Late time = MAX(0, Actual In - 08:00).
Use 08:00 as the current default; keep shift/calendar rule configurable.
Weekday single OT
Monday-Friday: OT starts when Out > 17:00; OT = Out - 17:00.
Automate after import.
Saturday single OT
Saturday: OT starts when Out > 13:45; OT = Out - 13:45.
Automate after import.
Sunday / double OT
Sunday: Single OT = 0; Double OT = total worked hours.
Automate after import.
Leave flag
If Day Remark = Leave, leave quantity = 1.
Import/validate leave, then reconcile with leave records.
Morning incentive
Rs. 200 when Actual In is present, day is not Sunday, and Actual In <= 08:00.
System calculates per eligible day.
Single OT pay
(Basic Salary / 240) × 1.5 × Single OT hours.
Use current payroll formula as configurable rule.
Double OT pay
(Basic Salary / 240) × 2 × Double OT hours.
Use current payroll formula as configurable rule.
No-pay deduction
(Basic Salary / 30) × No-pay days.
Automate from approved no-pay days.
Late deduction
((Basic / 30) / 8) × MAX(total late hours - 1.5 hours, 0).
Current workbook effectively gives 1.5 hours monthly late grace before deduction; confirm HR approval before go-live.
Food deduction
Daily food mark × Rs. 100; monthly total is summed by employee.
Import/capture daily food and deduct monthly total.
EPF employee
8% calculated in current workbook on Basic less No-pay and Late deductions.
Replicate only after Finance/HR confirms the approved EPF base.
EPF employer
12% on the same workbook basis.
Configurable rate/base.
ETF employer
3% on the same workbook basis.
Configurable rate/base.
Other payroll items
Performance incentive, mobile incentive, vehicle reimbursement, food reimbursement, attendance incentive, production incentive, salary arrears, advance, staff loan, purchases, PAYE deduction.
Maintain as payroll earning/deduction components with approval and audit history.
Important validation points
The salary workbook contains an attendance-incentive note showing “Total: 2000”, “0.5 Leave: 0”, “1 Leave: 1500”, “2 Leave: 3000”, and “3 Leave: 4500”. Because this progression is not self-explanatory, the system should not hard-code it until HR explains whether these values represent incentive, deduction, or a special exception rule.
Standard working hours
The attendance workbook contains varying “Stand Work hrs” values across dates/employees. Therefore, the new system should use a configurable work calendar/employee shift rather than hard-coding one standard number for every day and employee.

9. Data, Security and Audit Requirements
One centralized online MySQL database shall be used for authorized multi-user access.
End users shall access the database through the application, not by direct database login.
Role-based permissions shall control view/create/edit/approve actions.
Key transactions shall retain created-by, created-date, modified-by and approval history.
Posted financial/payroll transactions should not be silently overwritten; corrections should be traceable.
Regular automated backups and a tested restore procedure are required.
Sensitive payroll and financial information shall be restricted to authorized roles.

12. Acceptance Criteria
A customer PO can be converted through sales order to invoice without retyping the same core details.
Entering sets correctly produces size-wise piece quantities based on the set template.
A full-set or partial-size return can be processed from the original invoice with stock and financial effects recorded.
Rep commission for a test invoice matches the approved formula: (Quantity × Rate) × (Received/Allocated Amount ÷ Invoice Amount), with commission blocked for late collections after 100 days.
The July-style attendance Excel can be imported with validation and produces an attendance summary.
Payroll test cases match approved current formulas for OT, no-pay, late deduction, food and statutory components after HR/Finance validation.
Management can view production quantity, production damage, damaged-goods stock, saleable stock, collections, payables, cash/bank and PO summaries from the system.
All critical transactions show who created/approved/changed them.
