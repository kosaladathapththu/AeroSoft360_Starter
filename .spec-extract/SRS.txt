1. Purpose, goals and scope
AeroSoft360 is a centralized, responsive web-based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) application to replace QuickBooks and disconnected Excel/manual workflows. A single MySQL database and a shared, double-entry General Ledger will connect suppliers, purchasing, raw materials, production, finished goods, sales representatives, customers, collections, banking, management reports and payroll.
1.1 Objectives
Reduce duplicated input from purchase orders, production records, customer orders and invoices.
Provide batch-traceable material and finished-goods inventory with production-stage history.
Support footwear models/variants and configurable sets with size-level stock control.
Provide auditable receivables, payables, cash/bank, tax records and financial statements.
Calculate representatives’ commission from eligible customer collections within 100 days.
Replace salary workbook calculations through approved payroll rules and attendance import/manual entry.
Offer decision-support reporting: manufacturing costs, budgets, variances, margins, cash forecasts and dashboards.

18.1 Acceptance scenarios
PO → GRN → material batch stock → production BOM issue → stage completion → finished goods → sales dispatch → invoice → receipt → GL statements reconciles by source and amount.
A 10-piece set expands into exactly the configured variants; a partial size return updates only eligible stock and correct customer credit.
A partial direct or rep collection allocates invoices accurately; rep handover does not duplicate revenue or AR settlements.
A cheque generates no earned commission until bank clearance; dishonour generates a reviewable correction with no silent post.
An eligible partial collection within 100 days earns proportional commission; late collection earns zero per confirmed rule.
Specific batch issue records the selected batch costs and reconciles stock and WIP.
Payroll imports detect malformed attendance and payroll results match approved reference cases.
Unapproved changes, self-approval and posting to locked periods are blocked server-side.
Exports match filtered posted transactions; backup restoration and financial reconciliation are demonstrated.
