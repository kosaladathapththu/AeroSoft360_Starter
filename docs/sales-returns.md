# Sales returns by invoice line

Open Sales > Sales Returns, or use Return pieces on an invoice. Choose the original itemized invoice, enter the received quantity for each specific article/size/colour line, choose Sellable or Damaged, enter the date and reason, then post. The confirmation includes a unique SR reference. View return shows every returned item and can be printed or saved as PDF; the return register exports a line-level CSV, and Reports includes a dated sales-return/credit-note report. Customer profiles have a Returns tab and include the credits in their exports and ledger reconciliation.

Quantities are whole pieces and can be returned in repeated partial deliveries, up to the original sold quantity per invoice line. The original invoice, item quantities and selling rates remain intact. A repeated submission key cannot post the same return twice. Closed accounting periods and invalid dates are rejected. Posted returns are immutable audit records. Original invoices and collections linked to returns are protected from edits/deletion that would break the credit-note trail.

Sellable quantities increase the matching finished-goods article and size balance and create a sales_return stock movement linked to the SR record. The existing stock model aggregates quantities by article and size; the return line additionally preserves the original colour and article-name snapshot. Damaged quantities are held in the return register and excluded from sellable stock. They are not silently written back into stock as usable goods. Serialized returns require the exact sold serial for each returned piece. Sellable serials become available again, damaged serials are marked returned_damaged, and open warranty claims must be resolved first. The return retains serial IDs for historical traceability.

Credit values use the original invoice rate and allocate its discount, charges and tax proportionately over the invoice item values. Integer-cent allocation and cumulative rounding preserve the exact original total across repeated partial returns. A zero-price piece may be returned without inventing revenue. Sellable and damaged returns both credit the customer under this workflow.

Credits post to Accounts Receivable (1100), Sales Returns and Allowances (4050, contra income), and Sales Tax Payable (2300) when tax applies. The invoice's unpaid balance falls by the credit; excess credit on paid invoices remains visible as customer credit in the customer profile and ledger. No cash refund, transfer of credit to another invoice, replacement order, stock-cost valuation or automatic earned-commission clawback is posted. Earned commissions remain for management review under the existing commission controls. Financial statements pick up the posted contra revenue; operational sales reports preserve original sales, and the separate dated returns report identifies their credits.

Imported opening AR balances do not contain article/size quantities and cannot support a traceable piece return. The two existing seeded invoices also currently have no item lines. The system explains missing item data rather than inventing sold quantities or stock. New itemized invoices created through sales dispatch, manual invoicing or POS provide the necessary links.

Only migration 2026_11_02_000002_create_sales_returns was applied. No business return was posted during verification, and the imported AR/customer balances were rechecked unchanged. Isolated tests cover partial/repeated returns, sellable/damaged stock, oversold limits, cent allocation, closed periods, transaction rollback, serialized units, customer-credit reconciliation, and historical aging. Browser automation was not used.
